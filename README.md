# Mobile-sales-dashboard-power-BI
Excited to share my latest Power BI Dashboard, designed to analyze ecommerce sales performance and uncover valuable business insights through interactive visualizations.

🔍 Key Dashboard Highlights:

✅ Total Sales Analysis – ₹43M+ sales overview
✅ Quantity & Transactions Tracking – 1K+ quantity sold and 205 transactions analyzed
✅ City-wise Sales Distribution – Interactive map visualization across India
✅ Monthly Sales & Quantity Trends – Performance tracking throughout the year
✅ Customer Ratings Analysis – Understanding customer satisfaction patterns
✅ Payment Method Insights – UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash transaction breakdown
✅ Brand Performance Analysis – Sales, quantity, and transaction comparison by mobile brand
✅ Day-wise Sales Trends – Identifying peak sales days for better decision-making
✅ Interactive Month Filters – Dynamic dashboard exploration
